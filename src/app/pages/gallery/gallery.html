SERIES: CATÁSTROFES

2026

Catastrophes happen to everyone. In moments of crisis, it can feel as though the whole world has become unfamiliar. Something within us kicks in and helps us keep going: denial, familiar automatic routines, optimism, or the first steps toward adaptation. The series explores the inner mechanisms of survival in catastrophes, both external and internal, and our ability to find support within ourselves or in the world around us when everything familiar begins to collapse. Sometimes we are stronger than we think.
